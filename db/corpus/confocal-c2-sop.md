# Confocal C2 Standard Operating Procedure

The Confocal C2 is a point-scanning confocal microscope in the Advanced Imaging Core. It
is the busiest instrument in the facility and is charged at $42.00 per hour at the
internal rate.

## Before you start

Confirm your booking is active in the scheduling system. Sessions cannot be started
against another user's booking; doing so misattributes the charge and is treated as a
policy breach.

Required training: Confocal Basics (in-person, half a day) plus current Biosafety Level 2
certification if you are imaging anything derived from human or primary animal material.

## Start-up sequence

Start-up must be performed in this order. Powering the lasers before the controller is
the most common cause of a failed session.

1. Switch on the main power strip at the wall.
2. Switch on the microscope controller and wait for the green ready light.
3. Switch on the laser unit key and turn the key to ON.
4. Allow the lasers to warm up for 30 minutes before acquiring any quantitative data.
5. Launch the acquisition software last, after the warm-up is complete.

The 30-minute warm-up is not optional for quantitative imaging: intensity drift over the
first 20–25 minutes is routinely 5–8%, which is larger than most effects being measured.

## During the session

Use the lowest laser power that gives an acceptable signal. Above 15% laser power on the
488 nm line, photobleaching becomes the dominant source of intensity loss in time-lapse
experiments.

Immersion oil is provided. Use only the Type F oil supplied by the facility; other oils
have a different refractive index and will degrade the correction on the 63x objective.

## Shutdown sequence

Shutdown is the reverse of start-up, with one exception: the lasers must be allowed to
cool.

1. Close the acquisition software and copy your data off the instrument PC.
2. Turn the laser key to OFF, and leave the laser unit fan running for 10 minutes.
3. Clean all oil objectives with lens tissue and the supplied cleaning solvent.
4. After the 10-minute cool-down, switch off the laser unit, then the controller.
5. Switch off the power strip and complete the log sheet.

Never switch off the power strip before the laser cool-down has finished. Cutting power
to a hot laser is the single most expensive mistake a user can make on this instrument.

## Faults

If the ready light does not turn green within two minutes, do not proceed. Log the fault
in the request system and contact facility staff. Repeated power cycling of the
controller can corrupt the stage calibration.
