# Billing FAQ

## How am I charged?

Instrument use is charged by the hour at the rate published in the instrument catalogue,
against the account code attached to the booking. Charges accrue against booked time, not
observed time, except where the facility's tracked-usage reconciliation finds a
discrepancy (see below).

## What is an account code?

An account code is the billing identifier belonging to a lab. Every user is assigned at
least one account code by their PI. A booking must carry an account code, and the code
must belong to a lab you are a member of — you cannot charge another lab's code.

## When are invoices issued?

Invoices are issued monthly, in arrears, in the first week of the following month. Each
invoice covers one account code for one calendar month, and its total is the sum of its
lines. A line corresponds to a chargeable item: instrument time, a service request, or a
consumable.

## Scheduled versus tracked hours

The facility records two independent numbers for every instrument:

- **Scheduled hours** — time that was booked.
- **Tracked hours** — time the instrument logged as actually in use.

These are reconciled monthly. Tracked usage with no matching booking is walk-up use; it
is charged to the user's default account code. Booked time that was never used is still
charged, subject to the cancellation policy.

A persistent gap in either direction is worth investigating: consistently booking more
than you use wastes your budget, and consistently using more than you book suggests
someone is running sessions against the wrong booking.

## Disputing a charge

Charges can be disputed within 60 days of the invoice date. Raise a service request with
the invoice period, the account code, and the line you are disputing. The facility will
compare the booking record against the instrument's tracked usage log.

## Rates

Internal rates are published per instrument and reviewed annually each July. External
users pay 1.8x the internal rate. Rates do not change mid-period: an invoice for a given
month uses the rate that was in force on the first day of that month.
