# Core Facility General Policies

## Opening hours and access

The three cores — Advanced Imaging, Genomics, and Mass Spectrometry — are bookable
between 08:00 and 20:00 UTC, seven days a week. Out-of-hours access (20:00–08:00) is
available only to users who hold a current after-hours authorisation, which is granted
by the facility manager and reviewed every twelve months.

Badge access is deactivated automatically after 90 days of inactivity. Reactivation
requires a short refresher with facility staff, not a full retraining.

## Who may use the cores

Any registered user with a valid account code may book instruments for which they hold
current training. Users without training on an instrument may still submit samples as a
service request, which facility staff run on their behalf.

External (non-institutional) users are charged the external rate, which is 1.8x the
internal hourly rate shown in the instrument catalogue.

## Cancellation and no-shows

Bookings may be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before the session start.
Cancellations inside 24 hours are charged at 50% of the booked time. A no-show — failing
to start a session within 30 minutes of its start time without cancelling — is charged at
100% of the booked time.

Three no-shows within a rolling 90-day window suspend booking privileges until the user
meets with the facility manager.

## Acknowledgement

Publications using data generated in the cores must acknowledge the relevant facility by
name and include the instrument model. The facility asks to be notified of any resulting
publication so it can be recorded in the annual report.

## Contact

Routine questions go to the core facility admin through the request system. Urgent
instrument problems — anything smoking, leaking, or alarming — should be reported by
phone to the facility duty officer immediately, and the instrument taken out of service.
