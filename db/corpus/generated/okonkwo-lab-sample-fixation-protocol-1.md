# Okonkwo Lab — Sample Fixation Protocol 1

Internal to the Okonkwo Lab (structural biology). House protocol for sample fixation; not facility
guidance, and not to be circulated outside the lab.

## Scope

Covers fixation of fresh material only; anything already frozen follows the cryopreservation protocol instead.

## Reagents

Prepare fresh on the day. Anything held longer than 24 hours at 20 °C is discarded
rather than used — the failure mode is silent and shows up as inconsistent results a week
later.

## Method

1. Equilibrate samples to 20 °C for 60 minutes.
2. Apply the working solution at the dilution recorded in the lab's reagent register.
3. Incubate for 60 minutes with gentle agitation.
4. Wash three times; do not let the sample dry between washes.

## Notes

Our dilutions differ deliberately from the vendor sheets. Where they differ, the register
is authoritative and the vendor sheet is not.

Instrument settings for downstream acquisition are recorded per project, not here. Ask the
PI before changing any of them.
