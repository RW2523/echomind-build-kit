# Booking and Cancellation Rules

## Session limits

A single booking may not exceed 12 hours. Longer experiments are booked as consecutive
sessions and require the facility manager's approval, which is granted for time-lapse and
overnight acquisitions on request.

Bookings can be made up to 60 days ahead. There is no minimum notice: if a slot is free
now, it can be booked now.

## Fair-share limits

During peak hours (09:00–17:00 UTC, Monday to Friday) a single user may hold at most 16
booked hours per instrument per week. Outside peak hours there is no cap. The limit is
per user, not per lab, so a lab with four members can collectively hold more.

## Cancellation

| When cancelled | Charge |
|---|---|
| More than 24 hours before start | None |
| Within 24 hours of start | 50% of booked time |
| No-show (not started within 30 min) | 100% of booked time |

Cancellations are made through the scheduling system. A cancellation by email or in
person does not count — only the system record does.

## Instrument status

An instrument in `maintenance` or `offline` status cannot be booked. Existing bookings
that fall inside an unplanned outage are cancelled by the facility at no charge, and
affected users are notified.

Planned maintenance is scheduled at least two weeks ahead and blocked out in the
calendar, so it never collides with an existing booking.

## Priority and bumping

The facility does not bump confirmed bookings for other users. The only exception is a
safety issue or an instrument fault, in which case the session is cancelled at no charge
rather than reassigned.
