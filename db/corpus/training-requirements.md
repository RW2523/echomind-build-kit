# Training Requirements and Certification

## General rule

Training is per instrument, not per facility. Holding Confocal Basics does not entitle
you to book the Cryo-EM Titan, and Biosafety Level 2 is a prerequisite layered on top of
instrument training, never a substitute for it.

## Certification validity

| Certification | Validity | Refresher |
|---|---|---|
| Biosafety Level 2 | 24 months | Half-day, in person |
| Confocal Basics | 24 months | Two hours, in person |
| Cryo-EM Operation | 12 months | Full day, in person |
| Mass Spectrometry Basics | 24 months | Two hours, online |
| Nanopore Sequencing | 24 months | Two hours, online |

A certification that has lapsed by less than 60 days can be renewed with the refresher
alone. Beyond 60 days the full initial training must be repeated.

## Booking blocks

The scheduling system blocks a booking outright when the user has no current training for
that instrument. It does not warn — it refuses. If a booking is unexpectedly refused, the
first thing to check is the expiry date on your certification record.

## Requesting training

Training is requested through the request system. Sessions run fortnightly and are capped
at four trainees per session for hands-on instruments, so book at least three weeks ahead
of when you need access.

New users who joined through the onboarding process are enrolled automatically in
Biosafety Level 2; instrument training must still be requested separately.

## Supervised access

A user whose training has lapsed may still run a session under the direct supervision of
a currently certified user from the same lab. The supervising user, not the trainee, is
responsible for the instrument and the charge.
